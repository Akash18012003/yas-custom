# YAS Commerce

Language for customer-authored product feedback and staff moderation.

## Language

**Rating**:
A Customer's product review containing a star score and authored content.
_Avoid_: Moderation record

**Visibility**:
The reversible publication state of a Rating: VISIBLE or HIDDEN. A Hidden Rating remains the Customer's record but is not published on the Storefront or counted toward the Product's average star score.
_Avoid_: Soft deletion
